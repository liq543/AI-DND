# NPCs: The Hollow Crown

> Each entry: role · look and voice · wants · quirk · secret · stat block · alignment. Recurring people from earlier
> chapters are marked *(from chapter 1)*. Truths behind the secrets are in `secrets.md`.

## The household of the Rookery (under the Grey Barrow)
### Mr Fennimore Quist, steward
- **Role:** the household's majordomo, hired in Gildford. He runs the house, the carts and the accounts.
- **Look and voice:**
  - Tall, stooped, about sixty, with a powdered wig he wears without irony.
  - Black gloves, a ledger under one arm, a soft accent from somewhere south.
  - Never raises his voice. "Very good, sir."
- **Wants:** a master worth serving, and 2% of everything.
- **Quirk:** polishes the Hall of Spoils' cases himself, and has opinions about their arrangement.
- **Secret:** he skims 2%. He has killed before, quietly, for a previous employer. He knows exactly what his new
  employers are, and approves.
- **Stat block:** `spy`. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

### Mother Hobday, cook
- **Look:** vast, deaf, with flour to the elbows. She talks only with her hands and her food.
- **Wants:** a kitchen no one interferes with.
- **Quirk:** feeds Nib like a son.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### The house servants
- **Ada and Bel Crisp:** maids, sisters, sharp-eyed.
- **Tobin Rudge:** footman, eager.
- **Corlis:** the mute stableboy.

All hired abroad. None have been below the Great Hall. All `commoner`, Neutral.

### Harl and Bastian, house-guards
- **Look:** foreign sellswords in good mail. Harl has a broken nose and is quiet; Bastian is pretty and talks.
- **Loyalty:** to the pay, which is very good.
- **Stat block:** `tough`. **Alignment:** Neutral Evil.

### Nib (16), Brakka's nephew *(from chapter 1)*
- **Look:** shot up tall and rangy in two years, all elbows and knuckles, his uncle's jaw coming in under a first beard.
  He wears a plain sword he isn't allowed to draw.
- **Wants:** to be treated as a man, and to be part of the crew.
- **Quirk:** practises the sword at dawn, alone, whatever the weather.
- **Stat block:** `commoner` for now; he'll get a real block when he steps up. **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral.

## Crowsfoot and the Marchwood
### Reeve Alys Brinn, headwoman of Crowsfoot
- **Look:** a broad woman of fifty, sleeves rolled, chalk on her fingers from tally sticks.
- **Wants:** the village alive through the war.
- **Quirk:** counts things under her breath.
- **Secret:** her son ran off to the Hanged Men last winter.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

### Brother Cuthbert, Crowsfoot's chapel priest of the Dawn
- **Look:** young, freckled, earnest; the chapel bell is cracked.
- **Wants:** to do some good.
- **Quirk:** keeps a list of the village's dead, and of the masons who "went to the war".
- **Secret:** he's noticed the Barrow track never seems to lead anywhere, and that the masons never wrote home.
- **Stat block:** `priest-acolyte`. **Alignment:** Lawful Good.

### Mercy Fulk, alewife of the Crow and Kettle
- **Look:** quick, sly, forty, with a missing front tooth.
- **Wants:** coin, gossip, and a husband who isn't dead.
- **Quirk:** knows everyone's business and sells it. Her commoner Training skill is Insight.
- **Secret:** she is one of **Brother Ennis's Watchers** (a silver penny a week).
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Amos Pettigrew, carter of Thornbury
- **Look:** a thick-necked carter of forty in a leather jerkin, his face broken up and his ribs strapped.
- **Wants:** his mule back, his pay, and never to drive the March Road again.
- **Quirk:** calls everyone "master", even the alewife.
- **Knows:** what the robbers looked like and said (secrets.md §6.1, "The robbed cart"). He is laid up at the Crow and
  Kettle (Day 1 onward).
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Garrick Webb, smith of Crowsfoot
- **Look:** huge, silent, burn-scarred.
- **Wants:** to be left alone.
- **Quirk:** sharpens the Hanged Men's blades under protest, and charges them double.
- **Stat block:** `tough`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Old Nan Thistle, hedge-witch of Ashpole
- **Look:** a bent crone in a moss-green shawl, her eyes very bright, smelling of crushed nettles.
- **Wants:** to serve the Grey Lady again, or to take her power.
- **Quirk:** calls the barrow "the Lady's house" and Kit "the new keeper".
- **Secret:** she is a **green hag** and the last keeper of the Socket. She has waited four hundred years.
- **Stat block:** `green-hag`. **Alignment:** Neutral Evil.

### Dunstan Cole, charcoal-burner of Ashpole
- **Look:** black to the elbows, with a cough like a rasp.
- **Wants:** his clamps left alone.
- **Quirk:** knows every track in the Barrow Hills.
- **Stat block:** `commoner` (or `scout` in the woods). **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Sir Gerold Ambry, castellan of Harrowgate
- **Look:** a red-faced knight of fifty gone to fat, in good armour that no longer meets at the sides.
- **Wants:** to pay his debts and keep his keep.
- **Quirk:** collects songbirds in little cages.
- **Secret:** he owes the Charter League 6,000 crowns, and has been selling the garrison's grain to the Hanged Men.
- **Stat block:** `knight`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral, sliding.

### Sergeant Dorran Lerner, Harrowgate's tax-sergeant
- **Look:** a lean man with a lean smile and a writ for everything.
- **Wants:** a cut.
- **Stat block:** `guard-captain`. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

### Father Gaspard, Abbot of Saint Wendrel's
- **Look:** plump, soft-handed, frightened.
- **Wants:** the abbey solvent and its secrets kept.
- **Quirk:** sends a lay brother each quarter-day to ask after "the gentlemen's health".
- **Secret:** he sold more than the barrow. Two years ago he sold the abbey's serpent-carved reliquary to a foreign buyer
  for gold. That was Asche, under another name.
- **Stat block:** `priest`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

## The Hanged Men and the outlaws
### Mother Gallows (Marta Gall), chief of the Hanged Men
- **Look:** fifty-five, one-eyed, the purple rope-scar round her throat worn like a necklace. A voice like a gate hinge.
- **Wants:** the Marchwood's roads, and fear.
- **Quirk:** hangs a fresh noose on the Gallows Oak for every chief who challenges her. Eleven so far.
- **Secret:** she's dying (a growth in the belly) and hides it.
- **Stat block:** `bandit-captain`. **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil.

### Jessamy Crook, her daughter
- **Look:** thirty, quick and pretty, with a crossbow and her mother's eye.
- **Wants:** the band, and more than the band.
- **Quirk:** keeps a tally of everything her mother owes her.
- **Secret:** she would sell her mother to the right buyer. She is a potential lieutenant.
- **Stat block:** `bandit-captain`. **Alignment:** Neutral Evil.

### Kester Brinn, a Hanged Man
- **Look:** nineteen, gangly, his mother's broad face under a hood he never puts down in Crowsfoot's sight.
- **Wants:** to be somebody who isn't the reeve's boy.
- **Quirk:** whittles little crows from beech twigs and leaves them where he's been.
- **Secret:** he was at the cart robbery on Day 0. His mother, Reeve Alys Brinn, doesn't know where he is.
- **Stat block:** `bandit`. **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral.

### Sir Lucan Mire, captain of the Antler Wolves
- **Look:** a Greencloak knight-deserter, handsome and ruined, his antler badge turned upside down.
- **Wants:** to be a lord.
- **Quirk:** drills his twelve men like it's still the army.
- **Secret:** he murdered his own captain at the Ashen March to save himself. He is a potential lieutenant.
- **Stat block:** `knight`. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

## The Regency (Kingsholt, Castle Gaunt)
### Ansel Vire, Lord Protector
- **Look:** fifty, silver-templed, beautifully mannered, in black velvet. He smiles with his mouth only.
- **Wants:** the realm saved (as he sees it), and ruled by him.
- **Quirk:** never sits with his back to a window, since the Lidless taught him who might be watching.
- **Secret:** he poisoned King Harlan. He's in Lidless debt.
- **Stat block:** `noble`, with `knight` bodyguards. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

### Princess Elinor, eleven, heir to the Antler Throne
- **Look:** thin, pale, black-haired, too quiet.
- **Wants:** someone to believe her.
- **Quirk:** writes letters to her dead brother and hides them in the Fane chapel.
- **Secret:** she saw Vire leave her father's room with a blue bottle.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

### Marshal Edmund Strake, commander of the Greencloaks and the royal army
- **Look:** sixty, grizzled, honest.
- **Wants:** the crown served, and the war ended.
- **Quirk:** prays at the old king's tomb every dawn.
- **Secret:** he doubts the king died naturally.
- **Stat block:** `warrior-veteran`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral.

### Master Corwen, the late king's physician
- **Look:** old and trembling.
- **Secret:** his diary records the king's open-eyed death.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral Good.

## The Thornbound (the Elderwild)
### The Briar Mother (Ondra Wychwood), archdruid
- **Look:** two hundred years old, small as a child, bark-skinned, her hair a crown of brambles. Her voice is the wind in
  dry leaves.
- **Wants:** the Thornpact restored, and the wood obeyed.
- **Quirk:** never steps on bare earth; roots rise to meet her feet.
- **Secret:** she holds the Antler Crown, and keeps Prince Aldwyn asleep under the Weeping Yew.
- **Stat block:** `druid`, plus a once-a-day *wall of thorns* staff, two `treant` guardians at the Heartwood, and
  awakened trees. **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral.

### Corvane Redthorn, war-chief of the Thornbound
- **Look:** scarred, tall, a mantle of living thorn on his shoulders, his face tattooed with bark-patterns. Laughs when he
  kills.
- **Wants:** the crown on his own head.
- **Quirk:** wears the antler-badges of the Greencloaks he's killed.
- **Secret:** he wants the Briar Mother dead, and needs a knife who isn't woodfolk.
- **Stat block:** `gladiator`. **Alignment:** Neutral Evil.

### Elowen, the Briar Mother's granddaughter
- **Look:** young, grave, green-eyed.
- **Wants:** Aldwyn awake and safe.
- **Quirk:** leaves white flowers at the Weeping Yew each dawn.
- **Secret:** Aldwyn is her lover, asleep these four years.
- **Stat block:** `druid`. **Alignment:** Chaotic Good.

### Prince Aldwyn, the lost heir
- **Look:** twenty-six, dark-haired, grey-eyed (rather like Kit), asleep in the yew's roots, his beard grown long.
- **Stat block:** `noble` (asleep). **Alignment:** Neutral Good.

### Vyrmalth, the green dragon of the Green Hollow
- **Look:** jade and black scales, a frill like rotten lace, a voice like a lady's at a ball.
- **Wants:** songs about herself, and gold.
- **Quirk:** collects ballads; she keeps two captive minstrels.
- **Secret:** she would betray the Thornbound for a better hoard.
- **Stat block:** `young-green-dragon` (CR 8). **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

## The Charter League (Gildford)
### Speaker Ilsabet Cordray
- **Look:** sixty, a widow, iron-grey, with a laugh like a purse snapping shut.
- **Wants:** a charter of independence, and the Holdfast silver.
- **Quirk:** remembers every debt to the penny.
- **Secret:** the League forged the coronation crown.
- **Stat block:** `noble`. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

### Hendrick Lusk, master goldsmith
- **Look:** nervous, brilliant, with burnt fingertips.
- **Secret:** he made the false Antler Crown.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Greta Hask, gem-broker of the exchange
- **Look:** shrewd, plump, with jeweller's loupes on a chain.
- **Wants:** the next big parcel of stones.
- **Secret:** her ledger describes the crew's gem-sellers, and the Lidless have bought a copy.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Alignment:** Neutral.

### Captain Brannoc Vaux of the Gilded Hand (800 mercenaries)
- **Look:** gilded gauntlet, gilded smile.
- **Wants:** the highest bidder, each season.
- **Quirk:** never breaks a contract mid-season, and always renegotiates at the end of one.
- **Stat block:** `gladiator`. **Alignment:** Neutral Evil.

## The Watchful Church of the Unblinking (the Lidless Mission)
### Mother Sabine Roux, Missioner of the West *(from chapter 1: the woman in dove-grey)*
- **Look:**
  - Still neat, still in dove-grey, now with the Lidless silver eye-pin and a lace veil.
  - Thinner. She doesn't sleep much.
  - **She has kept her eyelids.** That's her one rebellion, and it will cost her.
- **Wants:** the Eye, or her life. Ideally both.
- **Quirk:** writes everything down, in a beautiful hand.
- **Secret:** the Choir's rakshasa is coming to collect her failure by Midsummer.
- **Stat block:** `spy`. In Act III use `assassin` if she's earned it. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.
- **Knows:** Kit's, Brakka's, Asche's and Ottilie's faces.

### Brother Ennis of Thornbury chapel
- **Look:** soft-spoken, kind to children, his eyes lidless and always wet. He blots them with a white cloth.
- **Wants:** to see God's eye open over the Hollowmark.
- **Quirk:** pays his Watchers in silver pennies stamped with an eye.
- **Secret:** his ledger of payments, including one "for the Chancellor's need".
- **Stat block:** `priest-acolyte`, with 4 Eyeless Brothers (`cultist-fanatic`). **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

### Vashti Ul-Amar of the Unblinking Choir
- **Look:** a woman of terrible beauty in white silk. Candles lean away from her and her fingers bend backward.
- **Wants:** the Eye for Oculon.
- **Arrives:** Day 90.
- **Stat block:** `rakshasa` (CR 13). Don't fight her fair. **Alignment:** Lawful Evil.

## The heroes *(from chapter 1)*
### Hedda Vask, paladin of the Order of the Unbroken Lantern
- **Look:** thirty-five, greatsword *Severance* on her back, her face harder.
- **Wants:** justice, and her name cleared. Kit holds the proofs.
- **Quirk:** still prays before every fight.
- **Stat block:** `knight` (a Paladin 8 build by Act III). **Alignment:** Lawful Good.

### Tobias Fenwick, scholar
- **Look:** his left arm and half his chest are green glass, which hums near the Eye.
- **Wants:** the Eye shattered.
- **Quirk:** talks to the sliver.
- **Secret:** he can sense the Eye through the Stillness.
- **Stat block:** `mage`. **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral, tending to Good.

### Brother Osric (Dawn war-priest) and Lysa Fen (Greencloak ranger)
- **Brother Osric:** `priest`. Lawful Good.
- **Lysa Fen:** `scout`, a sharpshooter. Neutral Good.
