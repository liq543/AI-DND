# The Story So Far: The Hollow Crown

## The Lost Years (two years between chapter one and chapter two; offscreen, agreed with the player)
The crew fled Vessarine over the Saltmarsh Causeway with the Ysmeran Eye and seventy cut gems. They spent most of a year
on the road east, through four kingdoms, fencing stones a few at a time. On the way Asche found what the Eye truly is
(a god's soul asleep inside it) and where it could be seated and turned inside out: the **Grey Barrow** in the Marchwood
of the **Hollowmark**, a forest kingdom torn by the Crownless War. They bought the barrow and its woods from the Abbey
of Saint Wendrel. They fenced 55 of the gems for 46,750 crowns and spent 25,000 building a lavish hidden lair beneath it.
They spent another 15,600 on gear and magic in Gildford's markets, and kept fifteen gems and about 4,500 crowns in reserve.
Asche seated the Eye in the old serpent temple's **Socket**, and the **Stillness** woke around the hill: no enemy can see
the lair or find a way in. They kept their hands in on the road and reached level 6. Nib grew to sixteen under Brakka's
sword lessons. Chapter one's clutter was thrown away; its trophies wait for the Hall of Spoils. The chapter opens on Day
1, in early spring of the war's third year.

**Days 7-11.** Mercy fed to the Eye at the new moon (the Stillness fed to Day 37); the voice in the Socket gave Kit statue-sight (one grey fleck). Through Ennis's own chapel face and a Mission-house face, Kit saw Ennis write to the Mother of Ears and recognised her as the woman in dove-grey. Tam broke under Corvin's Suggestion (Ennis's Watchers named). Lerner bought for 100 crowns on Day 7. Day 8: a ruse with Tam outside Thornbury went sideways: Tam and four Eyeless Brothers dead, the West Gate blown in by Fireball with its watch, Ennis taken alive from his chapel (his ledger burned; letters from "M." taken), nine Greencloaks and their sergeant burned on the March Road, the toll bridge burned. Sir Gerold's writ names Corvell a traitor. Hesketh offered his loyalty for the Hanged Men's chair; on Day 9 Kit cut Jess at his stirrup, Hesketh's man killed her, and Kit installed Hesketh as chief (a third weekly; 113 crowns paid now). Mother Gallows buried Jess at the Gallows Oak. Day 11, 08:32: Lerner's twelve and the Marchwood tax wagon are coming east through Crowsfoot toward Harrowgate.

**Days 11-15.** The Unkindness ambushed Lerner's tax wagon on the road with a felled beech and Fireball, took the tax and spared Sergeant Dorran, who was sent back out as a collector. Quist confessed his skim and lost two fingers to Kit's dagger. In Thornbury, Kit and Ottilie robbed the League house by night: the factor, his household and the porter were killed, and the strongbox, the seal, the bond-books and an assignment of about 4,000 crowns of Marchwood bonds were taken. Ottilie forged on the ride to Coldharbour, where Kit bought the inn with Pell's 2,000-crown note and paid for improvements. The gang collected the bonds: the Aldersons settled for cash. At Oxbow Mill the miller lied, so Brakka beheaded him, and Kit killed his wife and the boy and signed it 'the Unkindness'. Thornbury refused them at the gate; Kit's arrows and Corvin's Fireball broke its watch, the reeve was dragged onto the Cornmarket at dawn, and the town bent the knee (a fine, tolls, dues, and the Hanged Men and Aldous to hold it). On Day 15, Nell Pratt was fed to the Eye for the Mouth of Stone. Kit spoke through a bust in Gildford and, with Ottilie's help, hired Captain Vaux's Gilded Hand: 100 men at 2.25 crowns a head a day, marching at dawn on Day 16 for Thornbury (about Day 23), with a month's pay of 6,750 due at the gate. The session ended at 21:36 with nearly an hour left on the candle.

**Days 15-21.** Through a statue's mouth Kit hired Captain Vaux's Gilded Hand, and Thornbury fell to the Unkindness. Sir Gerold yielded Harrowgate, and Sergeant Lerner became Kit's tax-farmer and drillmaster. The crew destroyed the Thornbound raiders at the Hartstone and put the Stag and a spearman in Thornbury's stocks to hang. Ennis was broken in the Pens and gave up the Mother of Ears: Mother Sabine Roux. Mother Gallows died at the Gallows Oak. On Day 21, at the Wheatsheaf, the crew took Sabine alive in a fight with her Eyeless Brothers. Kit shot Landlord Fenn on the stable roof, and the inn burned. Sabine and the Watcher Goody Prail went to the Pens.

**Days 22-24.** Peg the serving girl and Ord the League clerk were hanged for the Mission. Davy, Fenn's boy, was found and spared to carry Ottilie's forged Sabine letter east. The crew reached level 7, and double XP became the house rule. The burned Wheatsheaf gave up Sabine's Robe of Eyes, now attuned to Kit. The Gilded Hand arrived on Day 23, and Ottilie talked Vaux down to half pay plus a fifth of all spoils. That evening Castle Gaunt's column (a banneret, two knights, sergeants and sixteen men-at-arms) yielded at the West Gate to Ottilie's parley without a blow. Twelve men-at-arms and both sergeants took Kit's pay, and the spoils armed and cheered every unit. Ivo got Darrow's +1 sword and a destrier, and the plate went to Quist to sell. Darrow went home on parole with a ransom letter, and Brey, Whitlock and four loyal men sit in the reeve's gaol under Hob Sallis, who is Kit's man first. At dawn on Day 24 Corvin's war council laid out the realm: about 140 blades, rich until Midsummer, the Regent's answer coming. Kit cancelled the Crowsfoot fort, and Fenn's ledger named the Mission's village Watchers, one of them a scullion in Kit's own Coldharbour. The session ended at 07:00 on Day 24, with the carrier due at 10:00, Quist waiting for Corvin's Sending at noon and the hanging at 15:00.
