Reviewed 2026-10-07 by the independent visual critic agent. The critic did not implement the art or change gameplay. This report stays inside the active campaign because it names its maps.

All 33 maps earn at least 8.0/10 for their current static visual presentation. Range: 8.0–8.4. Arithmetic mean: 8.1424/10. There are no remaining static-map blockers against the requested 8/10 standard.

Evidence: each map's actual browser overview and four distinct, verified camera sections in `all-maps/` (165 screenshots), inspected individually across the final capture batches. Earlier failing captures and repeated camera sections were superseded. The last batch rechecked maps 15–21, 26 and 30 after the final roof, furniture, street and wall fixes. Scores for previously passing current captures were retained.

Rubric: surface/architecture craft 2.5; prop/furniture identity and scale 2; lighting/weather/fog fidelity 2; composition/tactical readability 2; coherent place character 1.5. Eight means coherent, attractive and readable at both overview and room scale, with no substantial rendering defect. It does not mean every aspect is bespoke or flawless. Sparse areas were judged against their actual map contents; extra gameplay objects were not required to decorate empty space.

## Why the previous blockers now pass

Long tables, counters, bookcases and pews have clear wood surfaces, fitted depth and distinct edges; elongated dishes and near-invisible bench slivers are gone. Streets and major plazas have visible cobbles instead of blank pale squares. Stone walls and the farm's explicit timber material now match the painted environment. Leafy hedge edges no longer resemble sharp vector capsules. Bridges have timber grain and edge depth. Fallen bodies have separate compact tags instead of overlapping full-name plaques.

The Cornmarket's former flat texture swatches now have opposing slope shading, visible ridge caps, hip lines and dark eaves. Its large roof masses read as architecture at the map's scale. The West Gate quarter and the town overview likewise benefit from roof volume and textured streets. Map topology and sparse contents still give some places a diagrammatic character, but their presentation now meets the threshold.

## Limits and remaining polish

This is a visual review of actual captured frames, supported by read-only renderer inspection. The reviewer could not operate a browser surface in this subagent. Animation timing, sustained FPS, responsiveness on weaker machines and all possible hidden-state permutations are not independently certified by still images. Roof masking and disclosure behavior observed in the supplied examples do not prove every possible state. Test results and measured frame/load times belong to the implementation agent's validation.

Nonblocking polish remains: some floor and roof textures repeat visibly at close zoom; macro town roofs retain the map's abstract massing; dense regional labels and shortened corpse tags require zoom or hover for full names; a few interiors are spacious and visually repetitive. These keep the maps around 8 rather than 9–10. The requested minimum is earned for every current map, without claiming those limitations are gone.
