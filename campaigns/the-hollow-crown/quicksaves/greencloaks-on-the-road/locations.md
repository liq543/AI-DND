# Locations: The Hollow Crown

> The world map `hollowmark` (120×90 squares, **2 miles a square**, about 240 by 180 miles) is the **source of truth**. It's
> always open on the table. Coordinates below are world-map squares. Every town and site there has the crew's knowledge as
> its journal entry. Hidden places (marked **[hidden]**) appear with `map discover hollowmark <id>`.
> Travel (SRD): 3 mph at a normal pace, 8 hours a day, so about **24 miles (12 squares) a day on roads** and half that in
> forest. Deep forest and hills take double. `travel --to x,y` works it out on the map.

## Roads (and the rivers)
- **The Pilgrim Road:** the west edge (0,71) → the Last Lantern → Thornbury → Tanner's Cross → Kingsholt. This is how the crew
  came in.
- **The March Road:** Ashpole → Crowsfoot → Harrowgate → Thornbury.
- **The Barrow track:** Crowsfoot → the Grey Barrow (about 3 miles). The crew's own; it seems to go nowhere to anyone else.
- **The Gallows track:** Crowsfoot → Gallows Oak (about 26 miles north).
- **The Abbey Way:** Thornbury → Saint Wendrel's Abbey → Bracken Ford (the way into the Elderwild).
- **The Old Wood Road:** Kingsholt → Highbarrow → Hunter's Rest → Bracken Ford.
- **The King's Road:** Kingsholt → Stonebridge → the south.
- **The League Road:** Kingsholt → Fernwick → Gildford → Wyke.
- **The Amber Road:** Gildford → Ambersell → the east edge.
- **The Gaunt Road** (Kingsholt → Castle Gaunt), **Oxley Lane**, **the Redfield Lane**, **the Marrowdale Lane**, **the
  Fallowfield track**, and **the Holdfast track** (Castle Gaunt → Holdfast Mine).
- **Rivers:**
  - **The Wend** runs from the Greyspine through the Elderwild, past Bracken Ford and the Crownvale, under Stonebridge, and
    south. It is forded at Bracken Ford and bridged at Stonebridge.
  - **The Harrow Brook** runs from the Barrow Hills past Crowsfoot's mill and south through the Marchwood.
