# Quests: The Hollow Crown

## Active

### THE GRAND OBJECTIVE: a crown for the Jackdaw
- **Goal (the player's):** start from nothing in the Crownless War and rise. By the chapter's end, Kit leads a power in
  the Hollowmark, a faction he took or built, and holds a crown: the true **Antler Crown**, or the throne of Kingsholt
  itself.
- **Known to the crew:**
  - King Harlan died three years ago. The Antler Crown vanished the same night.
  - The Regency (Lord Protector Vire, ruling for Princess Elinor) holds Kingsholt and the Crownvale.
  - The Thornbound hold the Elderwild.
  - The Charter League's towns sell to both.
  - A foreign Watchful Church is rising.
  - The war has been a stalemate for a year.
- **Paths open (none chosen yet):** build your own (the Marchwood outlaws), take one over, play them against each other,
  or all three.

## Rumours and hooks (what the crew has heard in two years)
- The **Hanged Men** of Gallows Oak take "road money" from every village in the Marchwood. Their chief, Mother Gallows, was
  hanged once and didn't die.
- **Thornbound raiders** burned Brindle for cutting a sacred oak, and are said to be back in the Barrow Hills.
- A **lost prince**: Aldwyn, the king's son, died hunting in the Elderwild four years ago, and his body was never found.
  Every tavern in the realm has a man who swears he's seen him.
- **The Midsummer coronation:** Vire means to crown Princess Elinor at Midsummer. With what crown, nobody says.
- The **Watchful Church** pays a silver penny a week to anyone who "keeps watch for God". Its priests have no eyelids.
- **A green dragon** in the eastern Elderwild takes a tithe from the Thornbound.
- **Old Nan Thistle** of Ashpole cures warts, curses husbands, and says the Grey Barrow is "the Lady's house".
- ~~**The deserter-knight Sir Lucan Mire** and his Antler Wolves rob the Pilgrim Road west of Thornbury.~~ **Done, Day 5:** the
  Unkindness destroyed them at Fallowfield in a night raid (Lucan refused a captaincy). Their quarters and toll-hoard are the crew's to search.
