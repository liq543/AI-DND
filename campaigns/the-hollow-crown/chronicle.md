## The chapters so far

### Chapter 1: The Meridian Job (Vessarine, a lagoon city of canals; seven days; levels 3 to 5)
- **Hired for the impossible.** Kit Corvell, "the Jackdaw", a thief who had called the fence Valentin Crane "Uncle Val" since boyhood, survived a Collector
  raid on the Night Market and lifted **the Magpie's Key**. Crane hired a crew to steal **the Ysmeran Eye** from the
  Meridian Museum at its Lantern Gala. The Eye is a gorgon's eye that turns whoever meets its gaze to stone; the Meridian's
  "statues" were its victims. Kit learned that Crane had sold people to the Curator for years, and burned Crane alive in
  his own gambling hall.
- **The old crew walks.** The first crew split over Kit's killing. Hedda Vask (a framed guard) disarmed him when he went
  for the captured Dunnock brothers, and Hedda, Tobias Fenwick (the Curator's cursed former apprentice), Old Mags and the
  turncoat Sergeant Varga left him. Bruno quit. Kit killed the Dunnocks anyway.
- **Kit kills Sera.** When Sera Lark confessed that she was the Curator's daughter and wanted the Eye destroyed, not sold,
  Kit killed her.
- **The new crew.** Kit recruited the crew who are with him still: **Brakka Holloway**, a prizefighter, with his nephew
  **Nib**; **Corvin Asche**, a forger-wizard; and **Ottilie Marsh**, a playhouse prompter, forger and actress who talks
  for him. They left a trail of bodies across the city: a vintner in the baths, a tailor and his apprentice, a fence and
  her bodyguard, a mill-owner and his wife, a stage-door keeper.
- **The heist.** The night before the Gala they went in through a loosened culvert grate. They charmed the Grand Curator,
  took her ring, beat the vault's lattice with Asche's Knock, and lifted the Eye. With smoked-glass visors down, they turned
  the Curator, the Master of Wards and Captain Thorne to stone and smashed them, caged six Wardens, Fireballed the dock,
  and rowed away unseen through the fog.
- **The double-cross.** At the Old Customs House on the Mole they sold the Eye to the foreign **Emissary**: Ottilie talked
  the price up to **70,000 in cut gems**. Then they killed him. The Eye's gaze turned one of his guards to stone. He was
  not human: the fingers inside his gloves bent backward at every joint. His letter of passage named him "the Envoy of
  the **Lidless Court**" of the Obsidian Coast. His watcher, **the woman in dove-grey**, escaped by boat and has seen
  their faces.
- **Gone.** They fled Vessarine over the Saltmarsh Causeway in a cart, under drugget rugs, with the Eye and the gems.

### The lost years (two years between chapters)
- **Over the world.** They travelled overland for most of a year, far east and inland, fencing the gems a few at a time.
  Along the way Asche learned what the Eye really is: **a god's soul is trapped inside it**. He learned that it could be
  seated and turned inside out to hide a place rather than kill.
- **The Hollowmark.** They came to the Hollowmark in the second year of its civil war. They bought the **Grey Barrow** in
  the Marchwood's Barrow Hills, three miles from the village of **Crowsfoot**, from the Abbey of Saint Wendrel, and spent a
  fortune building a lavish hidden lair under it. Asche seated the Eye in its heart, and the **Stillness** (the ward) came
  alive around the hill.
- **Ready for war.** They kept their hands in on the road (they're level 6 now), bought magic in Gildford's markets, and
  learned the land, the war and its players.
- **Nib grew up.** He's sixteen, tall, trained in the practice yard by his uncle, and tired of being treated as a boy.
- **Retcon (player's choice):** the statue called "The Thief" in the Meridian was **never Kit's mother**. That thread is
  gone. Kit's mother is long dead and matters to no one; he still wears her magpie-feather pin. Kit likes it that the
  Meridian's victims are still stone. His one regret is not smashing a few and keeping them as pet rocks.

## The crew: who they are now, and what they will never forget
- **Kit Corvell (Rogue 6):** about thirty. A narcissist and a sociopath with taste. He plans to be a god-emperor and sees
  no reason he shouldn't be.
  - **He never forgets** that he walked out of the unrobbable museum with a god in his coat, and that the Emissary
    thought he could dictate terms to him.
  - **He trusts** his crew, and almost no one else. He has never lied to any of them.
- **Brakka Holloway (Fighter 6):** the Anchor. An orc prizefighter, loyal to Kit to the bone, and devoted to Nib.
  - **He never forgets** that Kit keeps his word. Kit put Nib up in the Silver Ewer as promised. When the Watch took
    the boy from there overnight, Kit had him out of the Lantern Hill watch-house the very next morning, released to his
    real uncle.
  - **His one old line** was to keep Nib away from the killing. Nib is sixteen now and wants in.
- **Corvin Asche (Wizard 6, Evoker):** forger, schemer, and the lair's engineer.
  - **Obsessed** with the Eye and the god inside it.
  - **Keeps** the Curator's stone finger in his pocket.
  - **Rarely raises his voice**, and has never lost an argument he cared about.
- **Ottilie Marsh (Bard 6, College of Lore):** the convincer. She does Kit's talking and forges his paper.
  - **She never flinched at murder.** She keeps the marks smiling right up to the knife ("the show must go on"), and she
    was in on every killing from the Alcazar audition on.
  - **She has played** every part from widow to dowager, and wants the one role she's never had: queen of something.
- **Nib (16):** Brakka's late sister's boy. Sullen, eager and restless, a sword on his hip he isn't allowed to draw.

## What they carry (the important things; the sheets have the rest)
- **The Ysmeran Eye:** seated in the lair as the heart of the Stillness. Moving it collapses the ward. Visors are kept
  for anyone who must face it.
- **The Magpie's Key** (Kit): the key that opened the Eye's vault. Its full nature is unknown.
- **The treasury:**
  - The Emissary's black lacquer casket, holding **fifteen cut gems** (about 15,000 crowns), in the Rookery's great
    strongbox in the Counting Room. Kit wears its key on a cord.
  - About **4,500 crowns** in coin, with Kit.
  - Everything else went into the lair and the crew's gear.
- **Lidless tokens** (Kit): the Emissary's black-gold closed-eye pin, his letter of passage, and three foreign gold "sun"
  coins.
- **Trophies in the Hall of Spoils:** the Curator's sun-and-key ring and her daybook, Thorne's smoked-glass visor, the
  gilded visor, a Warden's sigil ring, the Gala seating chart, and Crane's black-gold coin. Elsewhere: the Curator's stone
  finger (in Asche's pocket), and Emeric Dane's last letter (in Kit's strongbox). The letter was Hedda's proof that Thorne
  killed Dane, and Kit kept it.
- **Gear bought in the lost years:**
  - Kit's elvenkind cloak and boots and his Ring of Mind Shielding.
  - Brakka's plate and Flame Tongue.
  - Asche's Bracers of Defense and Pearl of Power.
  - Ottilie's Hat of Disguise, Eyes of Charming and luckstone.
  - Healing potions, riding horses for all five, and the old cart and bay.

## Who wants them, and why
- **Vessarine:** 5,000 crowns posted on the Curator's murderers. The descriptions: a dark-haired rogue answering to
  "Kit", a huge orc ("Brakka"), a thin clerkish man, a red-haired woman. It's half a world away now.
- **The Lidless Court** of the Obsidian Coast: their Envoy is dead and the Eye they paid for is gone. The woman in dove-grey
  knows all four faces.
- **The Hollowmark:** no one yet. To the Marchwood they are rich foreigners who bought a haunted hill. Two years of
  gem-selling may have left a trail.

## Promises, debts and grudges
- The crew owes no one in the Hollowmark anything, yet.
- **Kit has never lied to anyone in this crew.** Brakka, Asche and Ottilie trust him absolutely, and he them.
- **Hedda Vask** left swearing Kit was rotten. She knows about the Dunnocks, and she knows Kit has Dane's letter. Kit may
  not know if she knows he kept it.
