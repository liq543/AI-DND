# Locations: The Hollow Crown

> The world map `hollowmark` (120×90 squares, **2 miles a square**, about 240 by 180 miles) is the **source of truth**. It's
> always open on the table. Coordinates below are world-map squares. Every town and site there has the crew's knowledge as
> its journal entry. Hidden places (marked **[hidden]**) appear with `map discover hollowmark <id>`.
> Travel (SRD): 3 mph at a normal pace, 8 hours a day, so about **24 miles (12 squares) a day on roads** and half that in
> forest. Deep forest and hills take double. `travel --to x,y` works it out on the map.

## Roads (and the rivers)
- **The Pilgrim Road:** the west edge (0,71) → the Last Lantern → Thornbury → Tanner's Cross → Kingsholt. This is how the crew
  came in.
- **The March Road:** Ashpole → Crowsfoot → Harrowgate → Thornbury.
- **The Barrow track:** Crowsfoot → the Grey Barrow (about 3 miles). The crew's own; it seems to go nowhere to anyone else.
- **The Gallows track:** Crowsfoot → Gallows Oak (about 26 miles north).
- **The Abbey Way:** Thornbury → Saint Wendrel's Abbey → Bracken Ford (the way into the Elderwild).
- **The Old Wood Road:** Kingsholt → Highbarrow → Hunter's Rest → Bracken Ford.
- **The King's Road:** Kingsholt → Stonebridge → the south.
- **The League Road:** Kingsholt → Fernwick → Gildford → Wyke.
- **The Amber Road:** Gildford → Ambersell → the east edge.
- **The Gaunt Road** (Kingsholt → Castle Gaunt), **Oxley Lane**, **the Redfield Lane**, **the Marrowdale Lane**, **the
  Fallowfield track**, and **the Holdfast track** (Castle Gaunt → Holdfast Mine).
- **Rivers:**
  - **The Wend** runs from the Greyspine through the Elderwild, past Bracken Ford and the Crownvale, under Stonebridge, and
    south. It is forded at Bracken Ford and bridged at Stonebridge.
  - **The Harrow Brook** runs from the Barrow Hills past Crowsfoot's mill and south through the Marchwood.

## Places by region

### The Marchwood (act one)
- **The Grey Barrow** (21,62), the crew's lair. See below and `secrets.md` §4.
- **Crowsfoot** (22,63), village (pop. about 250):
  - **The green**, with a lightning-split oak.
  - **The Crow and Kettle alehouse**, kept by Mercy Fulk.
  - **The smithy**, Garrick Webb's.
  - **The mill on the Harrow Brook.**
  - **The chapel of the Dawn**, with a cracked bell, kept by Brother Cuthbert.
  - **The reeve's longhouse**, Alys Brinn's.
  - **The post-box at the Barrow track's end:** a weathered box on a pole where the carts unload and letters for "the
    Gentlemen of the Grey Barrow" are left.
- **Ashpole** (13,67), hamlet: charcoal clamps and huts. Old Nan Thistle's hut with its carved hearthstone; Dunstan Cole.
- **Harrowgate** (31,67), castle:
  - A square keep on a crag over the March Road: a curtain wall, a gatehouse with a portcullis, and a well.
  - About 25 men: 8 Greencloaks, conscripts, and Sergeant Dorran Lerner.
  - Sir Gerold Ambry's songbirds in the solar.
- **Thornbury** (37,74), town (pop. about 2,000):
  - A timber palisade.
  - The **Wheatsheaf**, the **Plough and Pennant** and the **Drover's Rest** (inns).
  - The **horse fair** field, the timber yards, and the **League factor's house**.
  - **The new Watchful chapel** on the square, where Brother Ennis keeps a ledger.
  - **The Dawn church of Saint Ebba.**
  - **The laundry at the Wash-Lane ford,** where Morwenna Sallis works.
- **Saint Wendrel's Abbey** (41,59): a walled abbey with granaries, an ossuary, and Father Gaspard's lodgings.
- **Marrowdale** (44,83), village: cider and cheese, and the maypole where they hanged a raider.
- **Fallowfield** (8,78), hamlet: half-empty farms.
- **The Last Lantern** (6,71): a walled inn on the Pilgrim Road where it climbs west out of the realm.
- **Gallows Oak** (27,50): the Hanged Men's moot. A hollow oak with nooses at a crossing of hunters' paths; their camps
  lie within a mile.
- **Burned Brindle** (14,56): the burned hamlet, chimneys standing. The Thornbound raid on it is still remembered.
- **The Hollins Stones** (22,61): seven serpent-carved standing stones, all facing the barrow. They were the Socket's
  outer ring.

### The Elderwild (act two)
- **Bracken Ford** (52,44): the stockaded ford on the Wend, and the Regency front.
- **Hunter's Rest** (60,41): trappers, furs, and Merriwick the fool's grave.
- **The Ashen March** (48,38): the burned battlefield of the Regency column. Its ghosts walk.
- **[hidden] Elderhaugh** (46,30): the Thornbound tree-town; the Briar Mother's court; Corvane's war-camp.
- **[hidden] The Heartwood** (55,21): the holy grove, the living thorn-throne, and the Antler Crown.
- **[hidden] The Weeping Yew** (40,17): Prince Aldwyn asleep in its roots.
- **[hidden] Ysm-Tharuun** (30,14): the dead serpent city and the Gorgon's Mantle.
- **[hidden] The Green Hollow** (72,24): Vyrmalth's lair.
- **The Sump** (36,30): a swamp of drowned serpent temples (its label is hidden until found).

### The Crownvale (act three)
- **Kingsholt** (86,52), the capital (pop. about 25,000):
  - **The Antler Hall**, the throne hall of the forest kings, with a living throne of antler.
  - **The High Fane of the Dawn**, where the false crown waits in the treasury.
  - **The Regency court** in the Old Palace.
  - **The guild halls** and the League townhouses.
  - **The Rag Gate slums.**
  - The gallows on Hangman's Green.
- **Castle Gaunt** (93,43): Vire's black castle, his treasury and his household troops.
- **Highbarrow** (70,47): the garrison town and the western army's winter quarters.
- **Tanner's Cross** (74,62): tanneries, the horse market, and Gristle's fighting pit.
- **Oxley** (80,44), **Fernwick** (96,60; the Gilded Goose inn), **Larkspur** (66,54).
- **Redfield** (63,57): the great battlefield, red-flowered in summer.
- **The Watchtower of the Unblinking** (82,57): the Lidless white tower outside the south gate.

### The League lands and the Greyspine
- **Gildford** (104,73), the League city (pop. about 12,000): brick walls, the gem exchange (Greta Hask), Arcanists' Row
  (where the crew's magic was bought), the halls of the Gilded Hand, and Hendrick Lusk's workshop.
- **Stonebridge** (92,80): a walled town at the great bridge.
- **Ambersell** (112,58): amber and glass, and the road east. The Seeing Glass comes this way (`secrets.md` §5.1).
- **Wyke** (100,84): downs, sheep, windmills.
- **Holdfast Mine** (88,8): the Crown's silver mine, worked by convicts.

## The Grey Barrow and the Rookery (the crew's lair; final)
Three maps, all to the detail standard (every object a point of interest; the full DM detail is in `secrets.md` §4):
`barrow-gate` (44×30, outside and the upper rooms), `rookery` (52×36, the palace), `socket` (36×26, the oldest level).
- **Outside:** a long turfed hill-tomb in yew-dark woods, three miles up the Barrow track from Crowsfoot. The Hollins Stones
  stand on the next hill east. The **Stillness** covers the barrow and a mile around it.

### The Barrow Gate (`barrow-gate`)
- **The forecourt of yews** (18,17), the great stone **tomb door** (19–20,16), the Barrow track out (20,25), the sentinel
  stone (25,20), the old well (14,22).
- **The decoy outer tomb** (x17–22, y12–15), with its robbed sarcophagus (19,13). The **serpent door** (19,11) behind it
  opens to the Magpie's Key.
- **The black-marble vestibule** (x10–15, y5–10): the serpent statue (13,7) and the household stair down (10,5).
- **The porter's lodge** (x17–22, y5–10): the porter's desk with Quist's gate ledger and the locked tray of grey tokens
  (18,6), the guards' bench, the weapon rack. Harl and Bastian keep it.
- **The servants' quarters** (x24–29, y5–10), **the servants' hall** (x10–15, y12–15), **the stores** (x24–29, y12–15).
- **The stable and cart-shed** in the hidden dell (x35–41, y8–15), round the mound by the path at y18: five riding horses,
  the bay draft horse and the cart. Corlis sleeps over the stalls.

### The Rookery (`rookery`)
- **North:**
  - **The kitchen** (x1–8, y1–7): the range, the bread oven, the worktable, the scullery. Mother Hobday's.
  - **The cellar and buttery** (x1–8, y9–15): the cask wall, the wine racks, the icehouse chests.
  - **The service passage** (x10, y1–15), with the serving door into the hall (11,8).
  - **The great hall** (x12–38, y1–15): the great hearth (25,1), the long table for forty on its carpet (x15–36, y6–10),
    **Kit's great chair** on the dais (37,8), the serpent columns, the candelabra, and the minstrels' gallery rail (y14).
  - **Kit's bedchamber** (x40–50, y1–7) and **study** (x40–50, y9–15): the great bed, the wardrobes, the map table (42,11),
    the writing desk, **Kit's strongbox** (41,14), and the secret door (45,16) to the gallery by the Coil Stair.
- **The long gallery, the Hall of Spoils** (y17–19, the whole width): plinths and glass cases (chapter one's trophies at
  (13,17)), the household stair up to the Barrow Gate (10,18), the serpent door (39,18), and **the Coil Stair** down (49,18).
- **South:**
  - **Ottilie's salon and theatre** (x1–13, y21–34): the little stage, the footlights, the gilt chairs, the costume racks,
    her forger's desk (11,32), and her chest of identities (13,30).
  - **Asche's library** (x15–27, y21–27); **workshop** (x15–23, y29–34: the workbenches, the press, the cauldron, the
    summoning circle, Asche's chest at (23,30)); **the cold room** (x25–27, y29–34: the slab).
  - **Brakka's yard** (x29–41, y21–34): the sand pit (35,27), the weapon racks, the benches, the trough, the dummy.
  - **The armoury and Nib's loft** (x43–50, y21–27).
  - **The Counting Room** (x43–50, y29–34): the vault door (46,28), the wage chests, Quist's ledger desk, and **the great
    strongbox** (49,32) with the Emissary's casket.

### The Socket (`socket`)
- The foot of the Coil Stair (7,1).
- **The Quiet Gallery** (x2–13, y2–23): the Twelve on their plinths down both walls, and two empty plinths waiting at the
  south end (3,22) and (12,22). A black runner down the middle.
- The passage to **the Socket door** (14,12), with the visor pegs (14,11).
- **The Socket:** a round chamber of green stone and serpent columns. The lead-weighted curtain (21,12) rings the
  black-iron cradle (25,12), where the Eye sits open, facing inward. The sealed serpent-door (25,19) leads to deeper dark.
