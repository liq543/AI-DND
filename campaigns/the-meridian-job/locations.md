### The Drowned Chapel — the Night Market (map `chapel`)
- **Where:** beneath the old drowned quarter; reached by the sacristy stair (from the streets) or the crypt stair (to the canals).
- **Description:** a half-flooded nave, floating candles, pews, an auction stage with a lectern. A lot storeroom
  (room 2) and a side office (room 3) lead to the crypt stair.
- **State when left (Session 1, 00:03):** raided by the Collectors; Wardens arriving. The side door is Webbed. Quill
  and a looter are still inside, along with the wrecks of two Warden-blades.

### The Drowned Canal Landing (map `landing`)
- **Where:** at the foot of the crypt stair; a forgotten canal under stone arches, with ledge tunnels running west and east.
- **Description:** black water, a deep central channel, a footbridge, glowing moss. Crane's gondola is moored here.

### The Gilded Heron (map `heron`)
- **Where:** Valentin Crane's gambling barge, moored in a hidden canal basin under Cinderwharf.
- **Description:** gilt and red velvet, card tables, a long bar, a smell of cigar smoke and river water. Crane's
  private salon is aft (room 2). A stair leads to the lower deck (room 3). The gangway is at the bow.
- **Notable people:** Valentin Crane, two bruisers, Ferro, the late-night gamblers.

### Tallow Lane, Cinderwharf (map `tallow`)
- A narrow canal-side lane of tenements and a chandler's shop (with a candle stall of stacked tallow out front:
  flammable). An outside stair leads up to Mags's room. There's an alley north, and a quay on the Cinderwharf Cut with the Collectors' skiff.

### The Sluice House, Gallowmere flats (map `sluice`, theme stone, dim)
- A derelict stone tide-mill about 1h40m by gondola from Cinderwharf, out on the reed-and-mud flats. The only deep-water way in is the old tide channel from the west, through a water arch into the mill race. On foot you can only reach it across the mud at low tide (north door 20,3; south door 23,16).
- Inside: the mill race runs through the middle with a plank boat landing (11-15,8). The great waterwheel is jammed at 18-19,9-10. The east sluice gate is rusted shut (25,8-11). A footbridge crosses at 22,9-10.
- North: the miller's room (hearth, cot, table) and the grinding floor (two millstones, stair to the loft at 24,4; part of the loft floor has fallen in at 22-24,7).
- South: the sack store (rotten grain), and the eel-trap room behind a rubble fall.
- The crew's new base from Day 1, 14:16 (Mags's suggestion).

### The Meridian — Public Galleries (map `meridian`, theme marble, bright)
- The Museum Landing on the Grand Canal (south). The grand doors at 16-17,18 are open in visiting hours (until the sixth bell, 18:00). Wardens check bags in the entrance hall (ticket counter to the west, cloak room to the east).
- The Grand Rotunda: a ring of columns around the Great Orrery (brass armillary and a great lens), behind a brass rail. Brass Wardens stand in the niches at 11,4 and 22,4.
- The Gallery of Benefactors (north corridor): portraits of the museum's patrons and curators. The locked staff door at 16,0 leads to the Orrery works.
- The Hall of Heroes (west wing): petrified heroes line both walls. Along the north wall are **seven new empty plinths**, which a stonemason is lettering "THE FOUNDERS".
- The Hall of Infamy (east wing): statues of villains and display cases. **The Thief** stands alone on a raised plinth at the head of the hall (29,3). A locked staff door (29,17) leads to the curatorial offices.
- The Acquisitions cut (the east canal): the water-gate is a portcullised arch at canal level beneath the east wing.

### The Acquisitions Dock (map `dock`, theme sewer, dim; linked to `meridian`)
- A vaulted canal-level dock under the Meridian's east wing. The channel runs in from the portcullis (east) to a turning basin (west). The north quay is the receiving floor: the ledger desk and the Receiving Ledger, a lamp, and a clerk's stool. A stair (25,2) goes up to the gate watch-room. A lift shaft (12,1) goes up into the museum. The iron door to the Undercroft stores (4,1) has a sigil plate and a Brass Warden in its niche.
- The south quay: crates of seized relics awaiting sorting, and a lamp at 6,16.
