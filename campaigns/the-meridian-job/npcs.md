# NPCs & Factions — The Meridian Job

> One entry per named NPC. Stat block = the SRD monster the engine uses (`npc add <slug> --name "..."`).

## Factions
- **The Meridian**: the museum. It runs the Wardens (guards), the Brass Wardens (animated armour) and the
  Acquisitions Office ("Collectors"), which seizes magic by Council writ.
- **The Gilded Council**: seven merchant-princes who rule Vessarine.
- **The Lantern Watch**: the city guard.
- **The Heron's Court**: Valentin Crane's fences, smugglers and gamblers. It runs the Night Market.
- **The Velvet Knives**: a rival crew of thieves.

## NPCs

### Valentin Crane, "the Heron"
- **Role:** the fence who runs the Night Market and the gambling barge the *Gilded Heron*. He was the Magpie's
  old fence, and he is "Uncle Val" to Kit.
- **Look & voice:** tall and thin, white suits, a silver eyepatch, a cane with a heron's-head handle.
  Soft-spoken, and calls everyone "my dear".
- **Wants / fears:** the Ysmeran Eye; being found out.
- **Attitude:** Friendly on the surface. **Stat block:** `spy`. **Status:** alive, not at the auction tonight.

### Grand Curator Lucienne Varne
- **Role:** head of the Meridian. Villain.
- **Look & voice:** sixties, silver hair in a severe coil, gloves always on, a warm and precise voice. Speaks of
  people as "pieces".
- **Stat block:** `mage`. **Status:** alive, at the Meridian.

### Collector-Sergeant Dunstan Varga
- **Role:** field leader of the Acquisitions Office. Leads tonight's raid.
- **Look & voice:** shaved head, a burn-scarred jaw, the Meridian's sun-and-key on his breastplate. Hums while
  he works. Testified against Hedda at her trial.
- **Stat block:** `warrior-veteran`. **Status:** at the Drowned Chapel.

### Captain Aldric Thorne
- **Role:** Captain of the Meridian Wardens. He framed Hedda.
- **Stat block:** `knight`. **Status:** at the Meridian.

### Inspector Halvard Quince
- **Role:** Lantern Watch investigator. Dogged and honest, and he already suspects the Meridian.
- **Stat block:** `guard-captain`.

### Mistress Dorothea Quill
- **Role:** the Night Market's auctioneer.
- **Look & voice:** a tiny, elderly woman in lace, with a gavel carved from a dragon's tooth. Drawls.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`. **Status:** at the auction.

### Lord Bertram Pellingham
- **Role:** a pompous collector, and Sera's mark tonight (she is playing "Countess Isolde of Varenne").
- **Stat block:** `noble`. **Status:** at the auction.

### Sabine "Silk" Delacour
- **Role:** leader of the Velvet Knives. **Stat block:** `bandit-captain`.

### Mother Aubrey
- **Role:** the old priestess of the Chapel of the Lantern Saint. Sera's mentor. **Stat block:** `priest`.

### Pip Vask
- **Role:** Hedda's twelve-year-old niece, in a Cinderwharf tenement.

### Ferro
- **Role:** Crane's gondolier. Waits at the canal landing below the Drowned Chapel.
- **Look & voice:** ancient, toothless, rain-cloak and a lantern on a pole. Hums sea shanties and never finishes a sentence.
- **Wants:** to deliver the crew to the *Gilded Heron*, and to get paid.
- **Stat block:** `commoner`.
