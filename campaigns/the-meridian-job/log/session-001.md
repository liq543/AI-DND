# Session 1 — The Night Market

- Day 1, midnight, the Drowned Chapel (map `chapel`). Dorothea Quill opens bidding on Lot 9, the Magpie's Key
  (catalogue handout shown: "removed from exhibit 'The Thief' during routine cleaning" — clue 1 of 4).
- The Collectors burst in through the sacristy door behind the stage: Sergeant Varga, 2 Collectors,
  2 Warden-blades. Varga: witnesses are "surplus". Combat starts.
- Round 1: Varga misses Hedda twice (he knows her — he testified at her trial). Tobias casts Sleep, which puts
  Varga and Collector B out. A Warden-blade crits Tobias (10/23); his concentration holds.
- Beta engine fixes mid-fight: Remarkable Athlete advantage (initiative and Athletics), spell save conditions
  taken from the SRD text, fliers using their Fly Speed.
- Rounds 2–6: both Warden-blades destroyed (Hedda crit, then Graze). Tobias dropped to 0, rolled a nat 20 death save,
  and re-Slept Varga. Sera's Hold Person paralyzed Varga. Kit's point-blank crit (27) brought Varga to 3 HP.
  Kit lifted the Magpie's Key from Quill with Fast Hands (26 vs DC 15). Hedda knocked Varga out on Kit's call.
  Both Collectors fled out the sacristy door: **Wardens are coming**.
- Quill recognized Kit: "You've got her eyes... You're the Magpie's boy."
- Beta fixes during the fight: Steady Aim economy, mastery per weapon, Shield on others' turns, Hold Person repeat
  saves, floor items, knockout counts as defeat, bystanders in initiative. The DM missed Tobias's Shield once;
  it was corrected by a public override.
- Kit's shortbow is on the floor at (5,4). Sergeant Varga is unconscious and captured.
- After the fight: Kit's quip ("a wizard, a witch, a convicted murderer and a thief walk into a church") earned Heroic
  Inspiration. Kit lifted the Codex of Stillness (Lot 11) from a looter (Sleight of Hand 12 vs DC 12).
  Tobias Webbed the side door (concentration). Quill and the looter were left behind, and the Wardens reached the chapel.
- The crew went down the crypt stair toward the canals. Crane's gondolier Ferro is waiting at the bottom.
