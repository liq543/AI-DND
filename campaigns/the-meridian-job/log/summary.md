# The Story So Far — The Meridian Job

> Compressed history. One short paragraph per session (5–8 sentences max), written at session end.
> This is what the DM reads to resume — detailed play-by-play lives in session-NNN.md files.

## Session 1 (Day 1, midnight → Day 2, 02:48)
Four strangers survived a Collector raid on the Night Market in the Drowned Chapel. Kit lifted the Magpie's Key, and the crew captured Collector-Sergeant Varga. Valentin Crane hired them to steal the Ysmeran Eye from the Meridian at the Lantern Gala (Day 8). They learned that the Hall of Heroes statues are petrified people, that the statue called "The Thief" is Kit's mother Marisol, and that Crane sold her out. After killing Lt. Brask on Tallow Lane and rescuing Old Mags, Kit burned the Gilded Heron with Crane and Ilse locked in its brig. Then the crew killed Ferro and two gamblers who had witnessed it, and moved to a derelict tide-mill, the Sluice House, where Bruno (lied to about Ilse), Mags and a hooded Varga now wait. Tobias cracked folio 32 (shatter the Eye while it's open and every sleeper wakes). He has drawn a line: no more killing the unarmed. The crew scouted the museum, and that night slipped through the Acquisitions water-gate on Brask's sigil. They turned the ledger, bought off the clerk Pim, copied his keys, loosened a culvert grate for Gala night, found the Carrow crates (Ashcombe is the Curator's man; the Order of Ceremony puts the Unveiling at midnight), located the Heart of the Orrery, and swapped a plain dagger for a still-unidentified Dagger of Venom. The session ends at 02:48 in the Undercroft Stores, with Pim's keys due back before 05:00 and the sigils dying at dawn.

## Session 2 (Day 2, 02:48 → Day 3, 11:17)
The crew slipped out of the Undercroft with the Dagger of Venom swapped and no trace left, and rested at the Sluice House. Varga agreed to testify against Thorne in exchange for passage out of the city. When the captured Dunnock brothers became a liability, Kit tried to kill them. The crew split over it: Hedda disarmed him, Bruno quit, and at the parting Hedda, Mags, Tobias and Varga left by gondola, leaving **Kit and Sera alone**. Kit then killed Col and drowned Wat (Uncle Rook comes looking on the night of Day 4). The pair caught a coal barge to the Gilt and conned their way into the **Rosewater Baths**. There Kit befriended the vintner Barnaby "Pell" Pellow, the Gala's wine supplier. Kit picked Pell's cabinet and stole his purse, a gold watch, his shop keys and the Meridian's **wine delivery order**: 60 bottles, Day 8, third bell, at the service landing, one porter admitted. Disguised as "Tam, Corvell's valet" (Sera talked Lettie round after Kit's bluff failed), he cut Pell's throat in private bath B. The session ends at 11:17 with the body cooling behind an OCCUPIED door, Kit (as Tam) caught in the foyer by the suspicious doorman Crabbe, and Sera working the Alderman's lady and her sister, who hold a Thursday supper invitation and gossip about the Gala seating.

## Session 3 (Day 3, 11:17 → Day 4, 08:27)
The crew went recruiting. At the Gutter Ring they won over Brakka "the Anchor" Holloway, a prizefighter, by clearing his debt to Dace and promising safety for his nephew Nib. On Quire Street they slipped past an Acquisitions watcher and brought in Corvin Asche, a forger-wizard. Quill fenced their hot goods. At the Rosewater Baths they conned their way in, robbed the vintner and killed every witness. At the Silver Ewer they lifted the tea merchant Ambrose Tull and robbed his room, the Peony. Sera, disguised as Tull, then cashed his forged drafts at Harrow & Vane for 300 crowns. Kit bought new clothes at Faircloth's, then came back to murder the tailor and his apprentice for the till. There Kit forced Sera to confess that she is the Curator's daughter and wants the Eye destroyed, not sold, and he killed her. Asche burned the shop, and the three bodies went with it. Brakka lost his bout to Black Sefton but kept his word and joined the party. Kit had put Nib up at the Silver Ewer, as he told Brakka *(the Watch took the boy from there overnight; see Session 4)*. The crew slept in Asche's hideout, the rag-mill loft on Vatman's Row. Next: Brakka wants to see Nib first thing, and the crew needs a new bard. Asche named Ottilie Marsh, the prompter at the Gilded Lantern playhouse on Lampblack Row.

## Session 4 (Day 4, 08:27 → 11:00)
Kit took Brakka to see Nib, but at the Silver Ewer the crew overheard that the Watch had taken the boy to the Lantern Hill watch-house overnight. *(Correction, made by the player in chapter two: there was no lie. Kit really did put Nib up at the Silver Ewer; the Watch took the boy from there overnight, and Kit got him out at once. Kit has never lied to anyone in his current crew.)* Disguised as 'Peregrine Lowe, clerk to Lowe and Strand', with Asche invisible, Kit talked the desk sergeant into releasing Nib to his real uncle, Brakka, who signed the day-book. Then Kit talked the escort constable into going home. Nib now boards with Old Wenna at her bakehouse on Chandler's Row (Brakka paid). At the Gilded Lantern playhouse Kit recruited the prompter-forger Ottilie Marsh (Bard 4) for 100 gp and an equal share. *(Her 'no murder' line was struck by public ruling on Day 4 at 14:27, and confirmed by the player in chapter two: Ottilie never disagreed with murder.)* When Kit's disguise dropped she recognised him from the Wardens' wanted bill. At the loft Kit briefed the whole crew: they will STEAL the Ysmeran Eye at the Blink and SELL it to the Emissary for 60,000, saving no one. He told them the old crew walked and 'the last bard is in the ground'. Ottilie forged the Lowe and Strand letter the sergeant was owed, and Brakka sent it by a Ropewalk porter. Next: contact the Emissary at the Blue Lamp on Gilder's Row, Ottilie's Alcazar Players pass angle (the masque rehearses inside the Meridian on Day 7 afternoon), and the wine route.

## Session 5 (Day 4, 11:00 → in progress)
Kit left a letter for the Emissary under the blue lamp on Gilder's Row, then woke the night clerk Pim Hobb at his lodgings and bought the Curator's schedule from him. The crew bought a cart, a horse, a rowboat and ten rugs. In the loft they murdered the mill-owners Jos and Hester Scrope and sank the bodies, then killed the Alcazar's stage-door keeper Mrs Danby and a witness in Lamplight Mews. On Day 5 the Alcazar company auditioned at the loft. Kit lifted Peel's Players pass for an hour and Ottilie traced it for forging; the plan is now to forge a letter from Whitlow adding four stagehands to the Day 7 rehearsal inside the Meridian. The Emissary replied: meet at 23:00 in the north room of the Old Customs House on the Mole, two of the crew, unarmed, with Crane's black-gold coin and the Magpie's Key. Kit killed the fence Quill, her bodyguard Gorse and Mother Tansy on Needle Row, looted them, and Asche set Tansy's shop alight. Brakka bought armour at the Arsenal Steps, the loot went to the pledge-broker Garrow, and Ottilie collected Pim's page of the Curator's movements. At dusk the crew scouted the Mole, where a lady in grey keeps a spyglass on the Customs House. Kit slipped inside and went over the upper floor: a swept north room with a table, two chairs and black cloth over the sea window, and a bundle of blank harbour customs forms in the clerks' room. Back at the loft at 20:06 the plan is set: Kit and Ottilie go in unarmed, while Brakka waits in plain sight with the cart at the landward end, keeping the crew's weapons in a bundle to bring in if it goes wrong.

**Session 5 (Day 6 evening to Day 7, 21:30).** The crew skipped the Gala and went in through the loosened culvert grate on the night of Day 6. Kit lifted Sergeant Mallory's sigil off the hook by Pim's desk while invisible, then the crew slipped into the Undercroft behind the Curator and ambushed her in Cage Four. It was a brutal fight: her Fireballs and a Cone of Cold put Kit, Brakka, Ottilie and Asche on the floor at different times, until Asche's Sleep, Ottilie's Suggestion and Kit's blades won it. Charmed, the Curator tossed Kit her own ring; they carried her down her stair, sealed themselves in her sanctum, levelled up to 5, beat the Orrery lattice with Knock and Brakka's back, and took the Ysmeran Eye from its vault. They tested it on her, and Brakka smashed the statue. With visors down and the Eye held high, they turned the Master of Wards, two Wardens and Captain Thorne (tricked into opening his eyes) to stone, smashed them, caged six Wardens, Fireballed the dock, cranked up the portcullis, poled out a stolen barge, killed the patrol skiff's crew, and rowed home unseen in the fog. On Day 7 the city is in uproar: the Meridian closed, five thousand crowns on their descriptions and on the names Kit and Brakka. Ottilie left the letter under the Blue Lamp ("Ready to make the drop. Same place."), Kit paid Pim his 200 gp, and Nib is back with Brakka. It ends at 21:30 in the loft, an hour and a half before the meeting with the Emissary at the old Customs House.

## Session 7 (Day 7, 21:30 → 23:43) — the end of chapter one
The whole crew went to the eleventh-bell meeting at the Old Customs House on the Mole, armed and with Nib, breaking the Emissary's terms ("If he wants the Eye, he can come and get it"). The upper floor was redrawn to scale before the fight. Ottilie talked his price from 45,000 back up to 70,000 in cut gems (and 5,000 for the Magpie's Key); Kit said "Deal" and the crew attacked. The Emissary read the double-cross coming, Fireballed the crew and dropped Asche, but Kit opened the Ysmeran Eye: one hooded guard turned to stone, Brakka's greatsword killed the other, and the woman in dove-grey fled blind down the stair to their boat. Trapped blind and poisoned at a swollen window with Kit blocking it, the Emissary died under Kit's Dagger of Venom, Asche's Fire Bolt and Brakka's greatsword; his split gloves showed fingers that bend backward at every joint. The crew took everything: 60 gems in the lacquer casket (Brakka), 10 in a velvet pouch (Ottilie), 514 gp (Asche), and from the body a black-gold closed-eye pin and a letter of passage naming him "the Envoy of the Lidless Court" of the Obsidian Coast. The Magpie's Key was never handed over. They walked off the Mole as the Customs House burned and the lighthouse bell rang, Ottilie bluffed the Saltmarsh Causeway toll-gate in a widow's cap with everyone else under the rugs, and at 23:43 they reached the mainland coast road with the Eye, 70,000 in gems and Nib. **Chapter one of the saga ends here.** The crew rides on to a far country; the next chapter starts at level 6.
