# Session 7 — 2026-10-02

> Play-by-play. Append a bullet per scene.

- 21:30 (Day 7): resumed at the rag-mill loft. Nib asleep by the stove; Asche oiling a writing case; Ottilie at the shutters; Brakka by Nib. The Eye under Kit's pallet. Meeting with the Emissary at 23:00, the old Customs House north room.
- 21:30-21:40: Kit: 'Everyone comes. If he wants the Eye, he can come and get it.' Brakka brings Nib ('not stopping in a loft on his own'). Asche notes the terms said unarmed; Ottilie notes they said two. Harnessed the bay in the mill yard; Brakka put on his splint.
- 21:40-22:25: by cart from Vatman's Row to the Mole (Ottilie driving hooded, Brakka and Nib under the rugs, the Eye at Kit's feet). Two Watch lanterns passed in the fog; none turned toward the cart.
- 22:25-22:33: cart left at the landward end. All five walked out along the Mole in the open, in by the unlocked side door, up to the north room: empty, candles burned to stubs. 27 minutes to the eleventh bell. Ottilie: 'Where do you want us, Kit?'
- 22:33-23:00: the crew waited in position. Asche lit his own tallow stub on the hearth ('His candles. I'd rather not.'). Brakka stood between the table and the door (4,3). Nib: 'Uncle. Is the man nice?' / 'He's buying, lad. That's nice enough.'
- 23:00: the Emissary's boat at the quay stair. Two hooded guards set down a black lacquer casket (3,6) and a small iron-bound chest (6,6) on the landing; the woman in dove-grey set her lantern between them (5,5). The Emissary stopped on the threshold (4,4) and won't cross it: 'Five. And a child. And steel, I think, under every coat.' He calls the broken terms a breach ('I am not offended. I am informed.'), wants the Eye set on the table, lids closed, before he sits, and says the 60,000 'may have shrunk on the stair' unless Kit can say why not. His eyes rested on Nib a beat too long.
