- **Goal:** at the Lantern Gala (night of **Day 8**) be inside **the Heart of the Orrery** when its iris opens at midnight (the Blink, about 3 minutes, all wards down), wearing a visor, and **shatter the Ysmeran Eye**. That frees every petrified "sleeper" (Kit's mother among them) and stops the Living Gallery (the Council petrified in the Ring, with Ashcombe spared).
