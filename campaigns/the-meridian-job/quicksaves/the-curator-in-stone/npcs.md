# NPCs & Factions — The Meridian Job

> One entry per named NPC. Stat block = the SRD monster the engine uses (`npc add <slug> --name "..."`).

## Factions
- **The Meridian**: the museum. It runs the Wardens (guards), the Brass Wardens (animated armour) and the
  Acquisitions Office ("Collectors"), which seizes magic by Council writ.
- **The Gilded Council**: seven merchant-princes who rule Vessarine.
- **The Lantern Watch**: the city guard.
- **The Heron's Court**: Valentin Crane's fences, smugglers and gamblers. It runs the Night Market.
- **The Velvet Knives**: a rival crew of thieves.
