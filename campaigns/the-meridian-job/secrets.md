# DM Secrets — The Meridian Job

> DM-only. Never reveal these directly; let the players discover them through play.

## The Truth Behind the Hook
- The **Hall of Heroes** statues are petrified people, turned to stone by the **Ysmeran Eye** (the preserved eye
  of Ysmera the Gorgon Queen, set in a gold and obsidian lens). The Eye is kept in the **Undercroft**, a vault
  beneath the Great Orrery. **Destroying the Eye restores all of its victims** and cures Tobias's glass curse.
- **The Magpie (Marisol Corvell)** is the statue labelled "The Thief" in the Hall of Infamy. She was
  petrified six years ago, on the night she broke in. She wears a silver magpie-feather pin, the twin of Kit's.
- **Hedda was framed.** Captain Aldric Thorne killed Lt. Emeric Dane because Dane saw the petrification.
  Thorne then knocked Hedda out and put her axe in her hands, and the Curator testified against her.
- **Tobias** was the Curator's apprentice. When he found the truth she cursed him with a sliver of the Eye.
  The glass is slowly spreading and will reach his heart in about two months (a narrative clock).
- **Sera Lark is Seraphine Varne, the Curator's daughter.** At 16 she watched her mother petrify the Magpie,
  and she ran away that same night. She knows the Meridian's servants' passages from childhood.
- **Valentin Crane sold the Magpie to the Curator.** In exchange, the Night Market got six years of
  protection. Tonight's raid is the Curator breaking that deal. Crane now wants the Eye, to sell to a masked
  foreign buyer called "the Emissary" for 60,000 gp, and he will double-cross the crew at the handoff.

## Villain / Opposition Plan
- **Who:** Grand Curator Lucienne Varne (`mage`), with Captain Aldric Thorne (`knight`) and Collector-Sergeant
  Dunstan Varga (`warrior-veteran`).
- **Wants: the Living Gallery.** At the Lantern Gala on the night of Day 8, during the Grand Conjunction at
  midnight, the Orrery's great lens will project the Eye's gaze across the Rotunda. The Gilded Council will be
  seated in the centre ring "for the unveiling" and turned to stone. Thieves get the blame, and the puppet
  councillor Lord Galen Ashcombe then grants the Meridian authority over all magic in Vessarine.
- **Clock:**
  1. Day 1: the Collectors raid the Night Market and collect loose ends (the Magpie's Key, the Codex of Stillness). **← now**
  2. Day 2: the Collectors hunt the escapees, and wanted posters go up.
  3. Day 4: Councillor Octavia Brisk, who is leading the vote to audit the Meridian, is lured to a "private viewing" and silenced.
  4. Day 6: the guard is doubled, the basilisk is moved into the Undercroft, and the Wardens are issued smoked-glass visors.
  5. Day 8, midnight: the Living Gallery.
  6. Afterwards: the Council is stone, and the Curator rules the city from the shadows.
- **Heist window:** during the Conjunction the wards drop for about three minutes ("the Blink"). Tobias
  calculated this years ago. It is the same moment the ritual fires.
- **Crane's clock:**
  1. Day 1: recruits the crew.
  2. Day 3: hires the Velvet Knives as a backup crew.
  3. Day 5: meets the Emissary.
  4. At the handoff after the heist: tips off the Watch.
- **Inspector Quince's clock:**
  1. Day 2: investigates the massacre.
  2. Day 3: identifies Hedda.
  3. Day 5: finds the safehouse, unless the crew covers its tracks.
  4. Day 6: raids the safehouse or offers a deal.
