# DM Secrets — The Meridian Job

> DM-only. Never reveal these directly; let the players discover them through play.

## The Truth Behind the Hook
- The **Hall of Heroes** statues are petrified people, turned to stone by the **Ysmeran Eye** (the preserved eye
  of Ysmera the Gorgon Queen, set in a gold and obsidian lens). The Eye is kept in the **Undercroft**, a vault
  beneath the Great Orrery. **Destroying the Eye restores all of its victims** and cures Tobias's glass curse.
- **The Magpie (Marisol Corvell)** is the statue labelled "The Thief" in the Hall of Infamy. She was
  petrified six years ago, on the night she broke in. She wears a silver magpie-feather pin, the twin of Kit's.
- **Hedda was framed.** Captain Aldric Thorne killed Lt. Emeric Dane because Dane saw the petrification.
  Thorne then knocked Hedda out and put her axe in her hands, and the Curator testified against her.
- **Tobias** was the Curator's apprentice. When he found the truth she cursed him with a sliver of the Eye.
  The glass is slowly spreading and will reach his heart in about two months (a narrative clock).
- **Sera Lark is Seraphine Varne, the Curator's daughter.** At 16 she watched her mother petrify the Magpie,
  and she ran away that same night. She knows the Meridian's servants' passages from childhood.
- **Valentin Crane sold the Magpie to the Curator.** In exchange, the Night Market got six years of
  protection. Tonight's raid is the Curator breaking that deal. Crane now wants the Eye, to sell to a masked
  foreign buyer called "the Emissary" for 60,000 gp, and he will double-cross the crew at the handoff.

## Villain / Opposition Plan
- **Who:** Grand Curator Lucienne Varne (`mage`), with Captain Aldric Thorne (`knight`) and Collector-Sergeant
  Dunstan Varga (`warrior-veteran`).
- **Wants: the Living Gallery.** At the Lantern Gala on the night of Day 8, during the Grand Conjunction at
  midnight, the Orrery's great lens will project the Eye's gaze across the Rotunda. The Gilded Council will be
  seated in the centre ring "for the unveiling" and turned to stone. Thieves get the blame, and the puppet
  councillor Lord Galen Ashcombe then grants the Meridian authority over all magic in Vessarine.
- **Clock:**
  1. Day 1: the Collectors raid the Night Market and collect loose ends (the Magpie's Key, the Codex of Stillness). **← now**
  2. Day 2: the Collectors hunt the escapees, and wanted posters go up.
  3. Day 4: Councillor Octavia Brisk, who is leading the vote to audit the Meridian, is lured to a "private viewing" and silenced.
  4. Day 6: the guard is doubled, the basilisk is moved into the Undercroft, and the Wardens are issued smoked-glass visors.
  5. Day 8, midnight: the Living Gallery.
  6. Afterwards: the Council is stone, and the Curator rules the city from the shadows.
- **Heist window:** during the Conjunction the wards drop for about three minutes ("the Blink"). Tobias
  calculated this years ago. It is the same moment the ritual fires.
- **Crane's clock:**
  1. Day 1: recruits the crew.
  2. Day 3: hires the Velvet Knives as a backup crew.
  3. Day 5: meets the Emissary.
  4. At the handoff after the heist: tips off the Watch.
- **Inspector Quince's clock:**
  1. Day 2: investigates the massacre.
  2. Day 3: identifies Hedda.
  3. Day 5: finds the safehouse, unless the crew covers its tracks.
  4. Day 6: raids the safehouse or offers a deal.

## Loose Ideas
- The Magpie's Key (Lot 9) opens the Magpie's old cache in the Tangle, which holds her notes on the Meridian.

- **Codex folio 32 (Draconic cipher), translated by Tobias in 1–2 days of downtime:** 'What the Eye has taken, the unmaking of the Eye returns. Shatter it at the moment its lids are open and every sleeper wakes, and every curse of its glass is lifted.' This is the core dilemma: Crane wants the Eye intact.
- The margin notes are in **Grand Curator Lucienne Varne's** handwriting. Tobias knows it. The Codex came from her private library; a servant stole it and sold it through the Market, which is part of why the Collectors raided.
- **Varga's sigil ring** (sun and key) is a real Warden sigil: it lets the wearer pass the Meridian's outer wards (legwork problem B). It does NOT track him; that was a bluff. It stops working if the Wardens notice he's missing and revoke it, which happens on Day 3 unless someone reports him 'on leave'.
- **Crane noticed Kit was hiding something** (Kit's Deception 10 vs his passive Insight 14). He suspects Varga talked. Clock: he'll have Ferro keep an eye on the crew, and he'll move up his meeting with the Emissary to Day 3.
- **Crane lied well** about 'the arrangement never touching the Magpie' (Deception 15 vs Kit's Insight 14). His buyer and his revenge motive are true, but only half the picture.
- **Forged leave request** (Sera, 20 vs DC 15): if it reaches the Master of Wards before the Day 3 dawn ledger check, Varga's sigil stays valid until the morning after the Gala. Delivery options: Old Mags Pritchard (the Warden post runs through the bridge gatehouse), or bribing a courier (risky).
- **The Magpie's Key matters to the Curator.** It is the one key ever cut to the Undercroft's inner vault lock (the Magpie made it from a stolen impression). The Curator wants it back 'whole' because its existence is a hole in her security. It will open the Eye's vault door, and she knows it.
- **The Collector-Lieutenant's Sigil** opens the Acquisitions **water-gate**: the canal-level receiving dock beneath the Meridian where Collectors deliver seized relics straight into the Undercroft stores. It's a second way in, and nobody has told the crew about it yet (Hedda can guess; Tobias knows the lattice around it). **Clock:** Brask's death is reported by Collector D today, so her sigil is struck at the next ledger check (Day 2, dawn), unless it's used before then or the ledger is tampered with.
- **Consequences of Tallow Lane (Day 1, about 10:30):** Collector D escaped and reports that Varga's ring is in enemy hands, so the forged leave letter is now useless for Varga's sigil. **Varga's sigil will be struck at the Day 2 dawn ledger check.** Mags is exposed as a collaborator and can't go back to the gatehouse. Hedda is named on a warrant. The Watch pulled Collector C out of the Cut.
- **Crane's secret is out** (Day 1, 10:50). What he did NOT reveal: that he hired the Velvet Knives as a backup crew (his clock step 2, planned for Day 3). He still intends to sell the Eye to the Emissary. His attitude to Kit: still fond, now ashamed, and so desperate to finish the deal that he's more dangerous than before.
- **Crane after the new terms:** humiliated and broke, holding a promissory note he can only honour if the Emissary pays. His next move (clock): contact the Emissary to move the handoff up, and quietly keep the Velvet Knives on retainer as insurance against Kit. He still loves Kit in his way, and will not order him killed. The Emissary might.
- **Crane's morning letter** (sent by Ferro before Tallow Lane) went to **the Emissary**: 'the crew is assembled, the key is in hand, I want the handoff moved up.' Now that Crane is locked up, the Emissary's agents will come looking for him and the key. **Clock:** they reach the Heron on the **night of Day 2** unless the crew moves or intercepts them. Ferro knows the drop point, a lamp-shop in the Gilt.
- **The Emissary (DM truth):** not human. A rakshasa-like fiend, or an agent of one, from the far-off Obsidian Court (keep the stat open; tier-appropriate agents first). He wants the Eye to petrify an entire foreign court, and he knows the Magpie's Key opens the Eye's vault. The candles leaning away and the no eating or drinking are tells. **Crane's jab at Sera** ('where you were at three this morning') is clue 1 of her secret night meeting. Clue 2: the damp coat hem. Clue 3: the rouge on the brandy glass.

## After the Heron fire (Day 1, 12:35)
- Crane and Ilse are dead in the brig. With Crane gone, the Emissary trail runs only through what Crane already said, Ferro (who ran Crane's errands and knows the drop points), and the letter Crane sent before Kit came back.
- Bruno and Ilse worked the Heron's door together for 6 years. If Bruno learns she was locked in deliberately, his loyalty flips (the 5,000 promise won't hold). He would go to the Watch or the Collectors.
- The Watch will investigate the fire. Gamblers A and B can describe the crew. The Heron's deed (held by Kit as collateral) now points straight at Kit.

Unnamed onlookers behind shutters may have seen a crew in a skiff. Bruno believes Sera's lie about Ilse, but if he ever finds her bones in the locked brig, it breaks.

## The Sluice House
- It isn't as abandoned as Mags thinks. The Gallowmere eel-runners (a small smuggling family, the Dunnocks: Old Aggie Dunnock and her two sons) use it as a drop every few nights. There's a fresh candle stub on the lamp hook in the sack store (14,12). A false wall panel (secret door 14,14; Perception or Investigation DC 15) hides their crawlspace cache at 11-16,15: casks of untaxed Ironmoor brandy, a tarred bundle of stolen Watch tabards, and 40 gp in a tin.
- The Dunnocks next come by boat on the night of Day 2 (after midnight). They're smugglers, not killers. They'll bargain, bolt, or fight if cornered. They'd make useful allies (boats, the flats, Watch tabards) or dangerous witnesses.
- The Watch investigation of the Heron fire: the clock starts now. The Heron's deed names Crane, and Crane's papers in the salon burned, but the Watch will drag the canal for Ferro's gondola, which the crew now has.
- Timing fixed: "the fourth bell" is the fourth bell of the night watch, **2:00 a.m. on Day 2**. The dawn ledger check is at about 6:00. The Sluice House to the Meridian's canal side is about 1h40m by gondola.

## The water-gate run (Day 2, 02:00)
- The gate watchman saw that the boat was a gondola (Perception 19), but bought Sera's story (Deception 9 vs Insight 4): Brask's crew, whose skiff was lost on Tallow Lane. He is lazy and stays in the watch-room unless something noisy happens (combat, shouting, the Brass Warden waking).
- **The Receiving Ledger** (on the desk): the sigil plate on the mooring post writes itself into the ledger in glowing ink: **"02:00 — Lt. O. Brask — sigil at the gate."** At the dawn check the Master of Wards reads this ledger. A dead lieutenant's sigil logged tonight tells the Curator someone used it. Tearing out or ruining the page raises questions but doesn't point to anyone. Forging over it takes Calligrapher's Supplies DC 15 (Sera or Tobias have them). The ledger also lists the week's receipts, including 'Lot 9 & 11, Night Market: NOT RECOVERED' and a note: 'Undercroft inner vault — Curator only.'
- **The Receiving Clerk** (asleep, a commoner, unarmed; Tobias's line applies) has a brass key ring: the stores' inner cage key and the lift key. Taking it quietly is Sleight of Hand vs his passive Perception 10 (he's asleep, so Disadvantage on his side: effectively passive 5).
- **The iron door to the Undercroft stores** (4,1): lock DC 15 plus a sigil plate. A Warden sigil (Varga's ring) or Brask's sigil quiets it. Without one, the Brass Warden wakes. Beyond it (next map): the stores and cages of seized magic, then the Undercroft stair down to the inner vault (the Eye). Tonight the vault is sealed and the Magpie's Key fits its inner lock, but the vault door's outer lattice only opens during the Blink.
- **The way back in for the Gala: the overflow culvert** (FOUND by Kit, Perception 21, 02:03 — secret grate 7,17 behind the relic crates; Perception or Investigation DC 15 to find). It's a rusted grate onto a disused outfall that opens into the Acquisitions cut below the waterline at low tide. Unwarded (the lattice ignores water drains). Thieves' tools DC 15 to loosen the grate, or Strength (Athletics) DC 18 to wrench it. Left loose, it's a sigil-free way in on the Gala night.
- **The lift** (12,1) rises to the curatorial offices behind the Hall of Infamy staff door. It's noisy.
- **Kit has the clerk's key ring** (02:05; Sleight of Hand 17 vs passive 5). STORES opens the iron door's lock (it still needs a sigil on the plate or the Brass Warden wakes). CAGE opens the stores' inner cages. LIFT works the lift to the curatorial offices. The clerk wakes at the 05:00 shift bell and notices at once. Clock: raise the alarm at 05:00 unless the ring is back on his belt, or he's given another explanation.
- **The alarm cord** under the ledger desk rings the bell in the gate watch-room and, through the ward lattice, the Warden post upstairs. If it's pulled: the watchman drops the portcullis (the gondola is trapped inside), the Night Wardens come down the lift within 3 rounds, and the Brass Warden by the stores door wakes.
- Kit handed Brask's signet to Pim (02:07). Pim saw through Sera's lie about the keys (Insight nat 20) and went for the cord. Kit spotted it.
- **Pim told the truth** (02:08). The stair behind **Cage 4** goes down to the Undercroft door: iron, with the Curator's own sigil plate. Only the Curator's personal sigil quiets it, so a Warden or Collector sigil there wakes the Brass Wardens. The Magpie's Key fits the inner lock beyond. The three Carrow-estate crates in Cage 4 hold ritual gala fittings: seven smoked-glass visors in velvet, and brass seat-plates engraved with the seven Councillors' names (Living Gallery clue).
- **Pim's deal** (02:09): the keys back before 05:00, and he saw nothing. He'll keep quiet on his own account (sleeping at the book), BUT Kit's threat failed (Intimidation 7 vs 15), so he isn't cowed. If the Master of Wards presses him at the dawn read about the "returned" signet, Pim tells the truth about the fake Brask crew (Master's Intimidation vs DC 12). If the keys are NOT back by 05:00, he reports them lost at once and describes Kit's face. The staff rolls are real: the Registrar's office above the Hall of Infamy (LIFT key; two night Wardens).
- **Pim is bought** (02:06; Persuasion 25). His silence now holds even if the Master of Wards presses him, as long as the keys are back by 05:00. He's a potential inside man for the Gala: delivery schedules, when the Curator comes down (she visits Cage 4 herself the night before any unveiling), and the Acquisitions rota. For more coin later he'd do small favours, but nothing that puts him in a cell.
- **Cage 5's sheeted figure** is Collector Aurel Venn, petrified three years ago when he tried a Collector sigil on the Curator's plate. It proves the plate fires the Eye's curse (a ward-trap charged from the Eye) on anyone without the Curator's own sigil. Shattering the Eye frees him too.
- **The Carrow crates, opened without trace** (02:12): seven centre-ring seat-plates (Ashcombe's has a sun-and-key on the back: he's the Curator's man); seven Warden visors plus Ashcombe's personal visor ('For G.A.: to be worn at the eleventh bell'); and the Order of Ceremony (11:50 the Council takes the Ring; midnight, the Unveiling). **Ashcombe will sit in the Ring wearing a visor, and be the only Councillor who walks away.** Count: seven Councillors (including Brisk and Ashcombe) and seven plinths.
